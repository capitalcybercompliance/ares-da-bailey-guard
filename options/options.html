    About Bailey Guard by CGA

    Bailey Guard by CGA warns you before you type your Microsoft 365 password into a fake sign in page. Bailey looks at every sign in page you open. If a page copies the Microsoft sign in screen but is not really Microsoft, Bailey blocks it and tells you why.

    Detection runs inside your browser. Bailey Guard does not collect your browsing history, your passwords, or anything you type. The only thing it downloads is the list of detection rules, so it keeps up with new scams.

    From Cyber Grants Alliance

    Cyber Grants Alliance is a nonprofit that helps small businesses and community organizations protect themselves from cyber attacks. Bailey Guard is a no cost tool we offer to anyone who wants it.
